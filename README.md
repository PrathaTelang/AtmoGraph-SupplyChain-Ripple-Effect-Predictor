# AtmoGraph – Supply Chain Ripple Effect Predictor

## Overview
AtmoGraph is a supply chain risk prediction project designed to understand how disruptions in one part of a supply chain can affect connected businesses and industries.

The project aims to combine Natural Language Processing (NLP), Graph Databases, and Graph Neural Networks to identify disruptions and predict their potential ripple effects.

## Problem Statement
Traditional supply chain systems often analyse businesses and events in isolation. They may fail to capture how a disruption affecting one supplier can spread across multiple connected suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, and retailers.

AtmoGraph aims to address this challenge through graph-based supply chain analysis and predictive modelling.

## Initial Scope
The first version will focus on a small, static supply chain dataset within the consumer electronics domain.

The system will gradually include:
- Supply chain data collection and preprocessing
- Supply chain graph construction
- News-based disruption detection using NLP
- Risk propagation and delay prediction
- Interactive supply chain visualisation
